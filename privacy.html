Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Quillara is a personal assistant used only by its owner. When the owner signs in with Google, Quillara accesses that owner's own Google data (such as Gmail, Google Drive, Docs and YouTube) only to carry out tasks the owner asks for.

  • Data is processed on the owner's own computer and is not sold, shared with, or transferred to any third party.
  • Data is not used for advertising or to train AI models.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Quillara's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
